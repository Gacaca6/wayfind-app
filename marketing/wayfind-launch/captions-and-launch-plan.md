# Wayfind · English launch campaign

## Campaign promise
Find a verse. Share the encouragement.
CTA for every post: https://app.wayfind.workers.dev
Publish manually on your own accounts. No advertising budget has been spent and no posts have been published by this workflow.

## Seven-day sequence
Day numbers start when you choose to launch; these are not scheduled posts.

| Day | Asset | Caption | Action |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Brag demo video + cover | A verse for what you feel. A place to share what you’re reading. Meet Wayfind. | Open the app and try a feeling. |
| 2 | Feeling-search graphic | Not sure where to start reading? Start with how you feel. Wayfind helps you find Scripture for that moment. | Try the Feel tab. |
| 3 | Kinyarwanda + NIV graphics | Read in Kinyarwanda, NIV or KJV. Open a chapter, take your time, and let the words stay with you. | Choose a version in the Bible reader. |
| 4 | Daily-verse graphic | Before the day gets busy, make room for a verse. Open Wayfind for today’s Scripture. | Read today’s verse. |
| 5 | Fellowship graphic | Reading can begin quietly and become a conversation. Explore community groups, prayer, and private chats in Fellowship. | Open Fellowship. |
| 6 | Bible-sharing graphic | Found a passage you want to talk about? Tap + in a conversation to share a verse or a photo of the Bible page you’re reading. | Share with a friend who uses Wayfind. |
| 7 | Demo repost / strongest graphic | What passage encouraged you this week? Read, reflect, and share the encouragement with someone. | Return to the app and keep reading. |

Append the CTA URL to the caption or place it in the platform's link field. Do not claim every caption link is clickable: use the profile link for Instagram and a link sticker for Stories when available.
